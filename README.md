##  Overview

**TagVault** is a next-generation campus utility designed to eliminate lost property anxiety at the University of Eastern Africa, Baraton. By pairing high-durability NFC stickers and encrypted dynamic QR codes with a lightning-fast cloud relay, students can protect laptops, keys, lab equipment, and textbooks instantly.

###  Key Value Propositions
* **Ultra-Low Entry Cost:** Students get a starter pack of 5 durable NFC/QR tags for just **KSh 50**.
* **Long-Term Reliability:** 4-year cryptographic tag lifespan with secure cloud data retention.
* **Privacy-First Anonymous Routing:** Finders scan the tag and contact the owner through a masked web relay—no personal phone numbers or emails are ever exposed.
* **Instant Rewards & Gamification:** Integrated campus karma system encouraging community return rates.
