Under the Hood of TagVault

Discover how our military-grade physical NFC hardware and privacy-first cryptographic backend protect your belongings across campus.

NTAG215 High-Frequency Chips

Our waterproof stickers house NTAG215 IC chips operating at 13.56 MHz. Compatible with 100% of modern iOS and Android smartphones without requiring any app installations.

Zero-Knowledge Proxy Relay

When a finder scans your tag, they enter a sandboxed web session. Your phone number, email address, and personal data are fully masked behind our encrypted proxy engine.

Dynamic Error-Corrected QR Codes

Printed alongside the NFC coil is a high-density matrix QR code featuring Level H error correction, allowing scans even if 30% of the sticker surface is scratched or dirty.

4-Year Cloud Registry Retention

Your tag keys remain active across your standard 4-year degree program. Cloud records automatically back up asset states across redundant database clusters.
